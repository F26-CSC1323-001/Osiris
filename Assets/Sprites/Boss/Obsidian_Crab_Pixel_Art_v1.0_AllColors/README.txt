OBSIDIAN CRAB - 2D Pixel Art Monster (8 directions, 5 colors)
Version 1.0

CONTENTS
  Purple/ Red/ Blue/ Green/ Gold/   one full set per color, each containing:
    Stand/     8 directions, 1 frame each
    Idle/      8 directions, 8 frames each
    Walk/      8 directions, 9 frames each (crawl loop)
    Attack/    8 directions, 9 frames each (claw raise and pincer snap)
    Death/     8 directions, 9 frames each (collapse)
  Previews/  showcase GIFs, 8-direction grids and an all-colors comparison image
  metadata.json  lists every state, direction, frame path and suggested fps

SPECS
  Canvas:      96x96 px per frame, transparent background
  Character:   approx. 58x45 px, ground line at y=78, centre at x=48 in every frame
  View:        low top-down (3/4)
  Directions:  south, south-east, east, north-east, north, north-west, west, south-west
  Frames:      frame_00.png, frame_01.png ... in play order
  Suggested:   Idle 8 fps, Walk 10 fps, Attack 10 fps, Death 10 fps
